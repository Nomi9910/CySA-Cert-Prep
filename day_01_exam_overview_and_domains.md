# Day 1 — CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-003) Exam Overview & Domains

## What Is the CySA+?

The CompTIA Cybersecurity Analyst (CySA+) is an intermediate-level cybersecurity certification that validates the skills required to configure and use threat detection tools, perform data analysis, interpret results, and identify vulnerabilities, threats, and risks with the goal of securing and protecting organizations.

**Exam code:** CS0-003  
**Level:** Intermediate (between Security+ and CASP+)  
**Experience recommended:** 3–4 years of hands-on security experience  
**Exam format:** Maximum 85 questions (multiple choice + performance-based)  
**Passing score:** 750 out of 900  
**Exam duration:** 165 minutes  
**Cost:** ~$392 USD (varies by region)  
**Validity:** 3 years (renewed via CEUs or retaking exam)

## Domain 1.0 — Security Operations (33%)

The largest domain. Covers the daily work of a security analyst:

- **1.1** Explain the importance of system and network architecture concepts in security operations
- **1.2** Given a scenario, analyse indicators of potentially malicious activity
- **1.3** Given a scenario, use appropriate tools or techniques to determine malicious activity
- **1.4** Compare and contrast threat intelligence and threat hunting concepts
- **1.5** Explain the importance of efficiency and process improvement in security operations

**Key topics:** SIEM, log analysis, network traffic analysis, EDR/XDR, threat hunting, CTI feeds, MITRE ATT&CK, SOC operations

---

## Domain 2.0 — Vulnerability Management (30%)

- **2.1** Given a scenario, implement vulnerability scanning methods and concepts
- **2.2** Given a scenario, analyse output from vulnerability assessment tools
- **2.3** Given a scenario, analyse data to prioritize vulnerabilities
- **2.4** Given a scenario, recommend controls to mitigate attacks and software vulnerabilities
- **2.5** Explain concepts related to vulnerability response, handling, and management

**Key topics:** Nessus, OpenVAS, CVE/CVSS scoring, patch management, OVAL/SCAP, risk prioritisation, attack surface management

---

## Domain 3.0 — Incident Response and Management (20%)

- **3.1** Explain concepts related to attack methodology frameworks
- **3.2** Given a scenario, perform incident response activities
- **3.3** Explain the preparation and post-incident activity phases of the incident management life cycle

**Key topics:** NIST IR lifecycle, PICERL, kill chain, MITRE ATT&CK, containment/eradication/recovery, digital forensics, chain of custody, lessons learned

---

## Domain 4.0 — Reporting and Communication (17%)

- **4.1** Explain the importance of vulnerability management reporting and communication
- **4.2** Explain the importance of incident response reporting and communication

**Key topics:** Executive reporting, metrics (MTTD, MTTR), SLAs, runbooks, stakeholder communication, vulnerability prioritisation reports

## CySA+ vs Other CompTIA Certs

```
A+  →  Network+  →  Security+  →  [CySA+ / PenTest+]  →  CASP+
                                         ↑
                                   You are here
```

- **Security+** — broad security fundamentals (prerequisite level)
- **CySA+** — blue team / defensive security, threat detection, analysis
- **PenTest+** — offensive security, penetration testing
- **CASP+** — enterprise-level architecture and design

---

## Exam Day Tips

1. **Read every word** — performance-based questions (PBQs) have specific scenarios
2. **Skip PBQs first** — come back to them; they take longer
3. **Watch for "BEST" and "MOST"** — these are judgment calls, not just facts
4. **Time management** — 165 min / 85 questions ≈ ~2 min per question
5. **Mark and review** — flag uncertain answers and return
6. **Process of elimination** — CySA+ loves "two close answers"
